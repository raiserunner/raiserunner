# RaiseRunner

Investor outreach for founders raising pre-seed, seed or Series A.

Upload your deck, confirm the facts RaiseRunner finds in it, and it matches investors to your round, writes plain emails from those confirmed facts, sends them from your own inbox and follows up on schedule. You see the replies and decide what to do next.

Prices are in USD. Cancel any time. No success fee.
